# Milestone 3: Check-In and Active Stays

Milestone 3 adds operational occupancy to the room board.

## Business Rules

- Guest name and vehicle plate number are optional.
- Checkout is calculated from the exact server check-in time.
- Payment is collected at check-in and copied into the stay record.
- Early checkout is allowed without an automatic refund.
- A room can have only one active stay.
- Maintenance and out-of-service rooms cannot accept check-ins.
- Unsupported room-type durations cannot be selected or submitted.
- A warning appears and sounds when five minutes remain.
- Overdue warnings remain visible and sound every 60 seconds until checkout.
- The employee must enable sound once per browser session because Android Chrome and other browsers restrict audio before user interaction.

## Deferred

Stay extensions, overdue charges, refunds, reservations, shifts, reports, receipts, and staff authentication remain outside this milestone.
